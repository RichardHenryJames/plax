PLAX - GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE: WHAT CHANGES FOR A RELEASE THAT CONTAINS GOOGLE ADS
Prepared: 1 and 2 November 2026, for version 1.6.0 (code 14), the first with ads. Nothing here has been submitted. In AdMob and AdSense
the app, the ad units and the consent messages were created on 1 and 2 November in the owner's logged-in browser (section 1 says which);
nothing was changed in the Play Console: that is your account. The official Google pages used are listed at the end.

PLAY-CONSOLE-ANSWERS-1.5.5.txt is still right for a 1.5.5 bundle, which has no ads. Every "No ads", "Advertising ID: No" and
"no permission but INTERNET" in it is wrong for a bundle of 1.6.0: use this file for that release.

State key: READY = taken from the code, check it once. DONE = already done, with the owner's login. OWNER = only you can do it or
decide it. HOLD = not yet.


0. WHEN THIS APPLIES
====================
To any bundle of 1.6.0 or later, with or without ad unit ids. The ads library (play-services-ads 25.5.0 and the User
Messaging Platform 4.0.0) and its seven permissions are in every build. A build that is given no AdMob ids shows no ads, and still
carries them, so Play sees an app that holds the advertising ID permission and the answers below apply either way.
.\package-aab.ps1 says which kind a bundle is: "shows ads: AdMob application id ca-app-pub-..." or "shows no ads: it carries
Google's sample AdMob id". The permissions it accepts beyond INTERNET are exactly these seven, and any other still stops the bundle:
  android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE            (play-services-ads-api)
  com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID            (play-services-ads-api: the advertising ID)
  android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID         (play-services-ads-api: Privacy Sandbox on Android)
  android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION   (play-services-ads-api)
  android.permission.ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS        (play-services-ads-api)
  android.permission.WAKE_LOCK                       (androidx.work, which the ads library pulls in)
  android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE              (androidx.work)
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, which androidx.work asks for, is removed by the ads library's own manifest, so it is not in the bundle.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE is an ordinary permission and the app starts no foreground service; if the Console asks about it, say so, and
look at the pre-launch report. READY, but only a Console run can show how Play treats it.


1. ORDER OF WORK
================
  1. OWNER  AdMob account (admob.google.com): accept its terms and enter the payment and tax details. These are yours to give. The
            account exists and its terms are accepted; AdMob still shows "Payment setup incomplete" until you do this part.
  2. DONE   AdMob > Apps: "Plax: Short News & Topics", Android, package com.plaxlabs.news, linked to the live Play listing
            (AdMob app 9585194942). It shows "Requires review" until AdMob has found app-ads.txt (section 5) and reviewed the app.
  3. DONE   Two ad units: a Banner "plax-banner-bottom" (ca-app-pub-2225161543339119/8383793916) and a Native advanced
            "plax-native-fullpage" (ca-app-pub-2225161543339119/5757630575). Both show "0 active" until the app is reviewed.
  4. DONE   AdMob > Privacy & messaging: the GDPR message "Plax app EEA UK consent" (EEA, UK, Switzerland) is published, with "Do not
            consent" on and the privacy policy address set. The US states message is not made; make it if you want it. The app asks
            for the message first and requests no ad until it is settled (GoogleAds.java).
  4b. DONE  AdSense (the website): the site plax-rouge.vercel.app is verified with the meta tag and its review was requested on 2 Nov
            2026, after the four NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_* variables were set in Vercel and the site rebuilt. Auto ads is Off for the
            site (so Google places nothing of its own over the stories); AdSense's "Auto optimise" is On, which is its default.
            The site shows "Requires review" until Google decides, which can take days or weeks; until then every ad request is
            unfilled, the strip stays closed and the page after the fifth story is dropped (two in a row, and no more that session).
  5. READY  Give the build the three ids (they are not secret, every APK holds them). In android\gradle.properties, or
            %USERPROFILE%\.gradle\gradle.properties, or as -P on the command line:
                admobAppId=ca-app-pub-0000000000000000~0000000000
                admobBannerUnit=ca-app-pub-0000000000000000/0000000000
                admobNativeUnit=ca-app-pub-0000000000000000/0000000000
            A malformed id stops the build. Then .\package-aab.ps1.
  6. OWNER  app-ads.txt (section 5), then the Console answers (sections 2 to 4), then the privacy policy switch (section 6).
  7. READY  Before any of it, look at the layout with Google's demo units, which show real test ads that are safe to tap:
                .\build.ps1 -Tasks ':app:assembleDebug' -ExtraArgs '-PtestAds=true'    (the debug build only)
            and to see the consent message as a reader in the EEA would:  -PadsConsentTest=eea  (or other).
            A live ad is never tapped while testing: that is invalid traffic and can suspend the AdMob account.


2. PLAY CONSOLE: APP CONTENT
============================
  Ads .................. YES, "my app contains ads". READY. (If the bundle shows no ads, the answer is still yes: the library is in it.)
  Advertising ID ....... YES. READY. Purposes to tick, the ones Google lists for its ads library: Advertising or marketing; Analytics;
                         Fraud prevention, security, and compliance. If this is answered No while the manifest holds
                         com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, Play refuses the release.
  Target audience ...... Unchanged, 13 and over, not children. Ads for an audience that includes children would need the Families
                         policy and a certified ad network; Plax is not that. In AdMob also set the maximum ad content rating and
                         block the categories you do not want next to news (for example gambling, dating, sensitive categories).
                         OWNER decides the levels.
  News and magazine .... Unchanged (section 9 of the 1.5.5 file). Ads do not change that declaration.
  Content rating ....... Answer the questionnaire again for this release; it asks whether the app shows ads.


3. PLAY CONSOLE: DATA SAFETY (change the 1.5.5 answers)
=======================================================
Google's own table for the ads library (version 25.5.0, the one in this build) says it collects and shares the following
automatically, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, encrypted in transit with TLS. The Console form is yours to decide
(Google says so); this is the mapping that fits it. READY to enter, OWNER to confirm against the form on the day.

  What the library says it collects         Data type in the form                       Collected   Shared     Purposes
  IP address (may give a rough location)    Location > Approximate location             yes         yes        Advertising or marketing, Analytics,
                                                                                                               Fraud prevention, security, compliance
  Taps, app launches, video views           App activity > App interactions             yes         yes        same
  App launch time, hangs, energy use        App info and performance > Diagnostics      yes         yes        same
  Advertising ID, app set ID, and other     Device or other IDs                         yes         yes        same
  identifiers of accounts on the device

  Encrypted in transit ........ Yes (TLS).
  Can the user choose? ........ The advertising ID can be reset or deleted in Android's settings, and Google's Limited Ads setting
                                 stops the ID and other data being sent. Answer as the form's wording fits you (collection by the
                                 library is automatic, not an in-app choice).
  Deletion .................... Plax's deletion (Account > Delete account) removes what Plax stores. It cannot remove data Google
                                 holds for ads; readers manage that in Android's ad settings and Google's ad settings. Say so
                                 plainly in the form's deletion text.
  What stays as in 1.5.5 ...... Name and email (with Google sign-in), the account id, the picture address, topics and saved stories.
                                 "No data is sold" stays true. "None is shared for advertising" does NOT: it is shared with Google.


4. STORE LISTING
================
Take out every "no ads" claim from the listing text in section 3 of the 1.5.5 file and from the Hindi draft: "No ads and no tracking
tools" is the line, and the short summary and the price line say "no ads" as well. Play shows "Contains ads" on the listing by itself
once the Ads answer is yes. Say what is true: Plax is free and has ads. Do not say the ads are "non-intrusive". OWNER.
The release notes should say that there are now ads (a strip at the foot and one full-page ad after every fifth story), and that the
reader is asked about ad choices first where the law requires it.

6. PRIVACY POLICY, AND WHEN
===========================
The policy is one page for the website and the app. It says that Plax shows ads, names Google as the one who serves them, and links the
reader's ad choices once NEXT_PUBLIC_ADS_POLICY=true (or the website has real ads). The website has real ads configured since 2 Nov
2026, so the live policy already says so (checked on the live page). Menu > Privacy and ad choices opens Google's message again, only
where Google says a reader must be able to change it.


7. WHAT WAS NOT CHECKED
=======================
Not checked, and not claimed: that AdMob or AdSense approves the app or the site (Google decides; an aggregator that republishes
headlines and short summaries is a case where approval is not certain); that Google Play's review accepts the full-page native ad
between stories (it is shown only when the reader swipes to it, has the Ad badge and AdChoices, and is left by a swipe, but the policy
review is Google's); how the ads behave on a physical phone (all runs were on an Android 16 emulator, with a fake network or Google's
demo units); and what any real ad looks like or earns. No ad has been served or tapped: the website's ad requests (made while it was
checked live on 2 Nov, with AdSense not yet approving the site) were all "unfilled".
