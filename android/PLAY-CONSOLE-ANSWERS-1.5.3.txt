0. IMPORTANT HOLDS BEFORE REQUESTING APPROVAL
============================================

HOLD A - Publisher content rights
Plax shows headlines, short summaries and pictures that come from publishers' public feeds
(for example The Hindu, NDTV, India Today, BBC, Al Jazeera, NPR, The Verge, TechCrunch,
Ars Technica, CNBC, ScienceDaily, Phys.org; in Hindi mode BBC Hindi, Dainik Bhaskar, NDTV
Hindi) and from open sources (Wikipedia, Hacker News, arXiv, NASA, the Met Museum,
Project Gutenberg, Open Library, ZenQuotes). Each card names its source and a button opens
the publisher's own page. Pictures load straight from the publisher's own server; Plax does
not host a copy (the phone keeps a short-lived cache, as any app that shows pictures does).
The code was not changed or tested to prove that every publisher's terms permit this kind of
use in a store app. Google's Intellectual Property policy is about rights, not about
attribution. Review each publisher's feed terms before you publish, and remove any source
whose terms do not allow it. Do not describe the content as Plax's own or as licensed
unless it is. [S25]

HOLD B - The news and magazine declaration is mandatory and its facts are yours
Plax describes itself as news and is an aggregator, so it is in scope of Google's News and
Magazines policy. The declaration needs your legal entity, entity type and a contact URL.
Google rejected the first version of this item: the contact URL must be a dedicated contact
page (now https://www.plaxlabs.com/news/contact), not the privacy policy.
See section 9. Do not submit entity facts you cannot back up. [S20, S21]

HOLD C - The mailbox and the account service must really be there
The privacy policy and the delete-account page promise that emailed deletion requests are
handled within 30 days. The mailbox must be yours and monitored. Sign-in and deletion also
depend on one free-tier Supabase project, which pauses after about a week without activity;
a daily keep-alive request is configured (vercel.json), and scripts\check-accounts.ps1
passed against the live project (Google sign-in, tables, keep-alive) while this sheet was
prepared.
A paused project means "Sign-in is temporarily unavailable" and deletion that cannot run.
Consider a paid plan before a public launch. See section 10. [S06]

HOLD D - The closed-test requirement may apply to you
If your developer account is a PERSONAL account created after 13 November 2023, Google
requires a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days before it grants production
access. See section 12. [S10]

HOLD E - Real Google sign-in from a Play-installed copy
Sign-in works through the system browser and Supabase (no Google Sign-In SDK, no Firebase,
no SHA-1 registration), so the Play app-signing certificate does not change how it works.
That was reasoned from the code and tested with a local stand-in for Google; a real Google
account on a Play-installed build was NOT exercised. Try it once in your closed test.

HOLD F - Keep every disclosure in agreement
The privacy policy, the Data safety form, the News declaration, the listing text and the app
must all say the same thing about: the optional account, the AI brief (written by AI on
request, labelled, can contain mistakes), the publishers as the source of stories, deletion,
and now the contact address (the same one in the app, on the website, in the store listing
contact details and in the declaration). If you change what the app collects or shows, issue
a NEW higher version code and update every one of them. Never re-upload a changed bundle under
a version code you have already uploaded: 1.5.0 was code 8, 1.5.1 was 9, 1.5.2 was 10, this one is 11.

HOLD G - The app is not available until Google approves the fix
Since 16 October 2026 Policy status says "App not available on Google Play". Nothing in the
code can change that by itself: steps 1 to 4 at the top have to be done in your Console and
the new release has to pass review. Do not say in the listing or to anyone that Plax is live
on Google Play until it is.

  outline, the colours, the icon sizes or the manifest drift apart, or a share card types a "P" again.
- The contact, privacy and delete-account pages were checked in a real browser, and live after
  deploy; the contact page is in the sitemap and every public page links to it.
- No full real-Google-account login on a Play build and no Play review has been done.

Rollout steps (for a new app; for the 16 October notice only steps 4, 7 and 8 and the top of this sheet apply):
 1. Create the app in Console (name, language, App, Free, declarations).
 2. Finish Main store listing (section 3) and the graphics (section 4); set the category and
    contact details (section 2), including the Store listing contact details.
 3. Complete every App content item (sections 5 to 9) honestly. The privacy policy URL, ads,
    app access, content rating, target audience, News declaration (with the contact page URL),
    Data safety (section 7), advertising ID and the financial/health/government declarations
    are the ones to expect. [S02]
 4. Upload plax-1.5.3.aab to Internal testing (or the track the app is on); accept Play App
    Signing; check the bundle's version (1.5.3, 11) and SHA-256 in Console.
 5. Install from the test track on a phone; read the pre-launch report and fix real problems. [S30]
 6. Run the closed test and apply for production, if required (HOLD D).
 7. Prepare the production release with the same bundle; enter the release name and notes.
 8. Review errors and warnings, then use Publishing overview > Send for review.
 9. Confirm the listing is really live. An upload, a draft or a test-track link is not proof
    that everyone can install it. [S11, S18]

Private keys and passwords must never be uploaded as listing assets, review attachments or
support files.
